2) Identify the action–reaction pair: A person standing on the ground

According to Newton’s Third Law, forces always come in pairs acting on different objects.

Action–Reaction Pair:

Action: The person pushes down on the ground

Reaction: The ground pushes up on the person


These forces are:

Equal in magnitude

Opposite in direction

Acting on different objects


This upward force from the ground is called the normal force.

✅ Answer:
Person pushes down on the ground; ground pushes up on the person.
